## Codebase Patterns
- **Pattern:** When adding new fields to `UnifiedRequest`, ensure they are correctly mapped in ALL provider request transformers (`packages/core/src/providers/*/request.ts`) AND format transformers (`packages/core/src/formats/*/request.ts`).
- **Pattern:** Use `createLogger({ service: '...', module: '...' })` for structured logging.
- **Pattern:** For Antigravity auth, always iterate through `ANTIGRAVITY_ENDPOINT_FALLBACKS` and handle failures gracefully.
- **Gotcha:** `biome` linting is strict about `any`. Use `// biome-ignore lint/suspicious/noExplicitAny: <reason>` when you genuinely need flexible types for protocol bridging or Discriminated Union workarounds.
